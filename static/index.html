Geostatistical Interpolation Methodology

1. Inverse Distance Weighting (IDW): Calculates ambient pollution as a normalized linear combination of nearby monitoring stations weighted by their inverse distance $w_i = 1 / d_i^p$.

2. Ordinary Kriging with Gaussian Variogram: Minimizes estimation error variance $\sigma_K^2 = \sum \lambda_i \gamma(s_i, s_0) + \mu$, accounting for spatial autocorrelation structures across the Pune urban terrain.

How to use: You can click on any point on the map to automatically populate latitude and longitude coordinates and compute the localized air quality index.
